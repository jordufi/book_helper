# Story Planner Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 26, 2026**

Story Planner is an app for designing novels (plot, characters, and chapters) that works **entirely offline**.

## What data we collect

**None.** Story Planner has no server, does not connect to the internet, and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking tools. Everything you write — books, characters, chapters, plot — is stored only in a local database on your own device. That information never leaves your phone unless you explicitly choose to export it (see below).

## Sharing information

Story Planner does not share any data with us or with third parties, because it does not collect any. The app includes an optional "Export" feature that generates a JSON file with a book's contents and hands it to your device's own sharing sheet (email, Google Drive, etc.); that action is initiated and controlled by you each time, and the resulting file only contains what you choose to export.

## Device permissions

The app may use the system's file picker to import or export your books as JSON. That access is handled by Android itself and limited to the file you pick each time; Story Planner does not gain access to the rest of your storage.

## Children

Story Planner is not specifically directed at children and does not collect information from any user, regardless of age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date above will be updated on this same page.
